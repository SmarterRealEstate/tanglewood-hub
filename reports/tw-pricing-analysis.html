The following analysis uses pricing provided by Brian/Medallion on September 15, 2026 as the reference point for each Terrace Home model. All competitor prices are net HST and sourced from active price lists. Comparisons do not account for standard features, structural options, bonus upgrades, or incentive programs being offered at each site.

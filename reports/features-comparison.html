Analysis Summary

Note on context: This comparison is preliminary work to inform decisions on inclusions, incentives, and pricing positioning. The audience is the Tanglewood sales team and Medallion; the goal is an honest internal picture, not a polished buyer narrative.

Where Tanglewood Is Ahead

  • Interlock driveway — confirmed as the only builder in the comp set. Every competitor with a verified driveway spec uses asphalt; this is visible and felt immediately on arrival.
  • Partially finished rec room included in base price — no competitor includes any basement finish in their standard price. Some are offering it as an incentive option (Minto, Dreamscape), which signals real cost in the buyer's mind; TW delivers it without the trade-off.
  • $3,950 all-in closing cap — provides cost certainty at close; one of the more buyer-friendly closing structures in the comp set.
  • Central A/C included — Minto HOH East is the only competitor that does not include it; TW's inclusion is a clear advantage over that community.
  • 12' main floor ceilings on select models — a Medallion Signature differentiator. No competitor in the comp set offers this at any price point.

Where Tanglewood Has Gaps

  • Smooth ceilings — the highest-impact gap. Tanglewood is the only builder in the comp set confirmed not to include them. Every other community includes smooth ceilings on at minimum the main floor, and The Views includes them full-house on all models. Buyers visiting multiple sites will notice this immediately.
  • Appliance package — cooking only (3 appliances). Homeward Hills, Winchester, and Dreamscape are delivering 5 to 6 full appliances. This is a direct comparison point buyers will raise on every visit.
  • Countertops — kitchen and bathroom — granite in the kitchen is solid quality, but quartz has become the expected standard for this price range. More importantly, TW offers laminate in all bathrooms with no stone in the primary ensuite; several competitors include quartz or cultured marble in the ensuite as standard. This is a gap that will matter to buyers comparing finishing levels.
  • No confirmed incentive program — most active competitors are running $10,000 to $40,000 in buyer-facing value. Phase 2's incentive structure should be defined before launch to remain competitive.
  • EV charger rough-in not offered — Winchester and Dreamscape include it; increasingly a baseline expectation in this price range.
  • Free assignment not available — Winchester and Dreamscape both offer it; a factor for investor purchasers.
  • Second floor and basement ceiling heights — 8' on the second floor (four competitors offer 9') and 7'2" in the basement (Winchester is 8', The Views is 9'). The partially finished rec room partially offsets the basement comparison, but buyers will still notice the height.

The Premium Benchmark: The Views

Jeffery Homes sets the highest interior specification standard in the comp set. Full-house smooth ceilings on all models, 9' ceilings on the second floor and in the basement (unique in the market), 10' main floor on 40' lots, gas fireplace, and glass shower are all standard. They are offering $10,000 to $20,000 in decor credits depending on lot width, and their co-op commission is at the market standard of 3%.

Pricing at The Views opens above Tanglewood at every lot width and extends significantly higher at the top of the range. The specification they carry justifies the premium in the buyer's experience. This is the community buyers doing their homework will use as the comparison benchmark.

What Buyers Are Asking For in 2026

The features comparison above reflects where Phase 2 stands today against active competition. Equally useful is what the broader market is telling us buyers expect in their next home. The following categories represent the most consistently cited buyer priorities in current market research.
Top 5 Tech Features
Smart Thermostat
AI-powered climate control that learns household routines is cited as the top expected smart home inclusion in 2026 surveys.
Video Doorbell + Outdoor Camera
Remote monitoring and real-time alerts have moved from premium upgrade to standard buyer expectation.
The Views includes this as standard.
Smart Lighting Controls
Automated and app-controlled lighting ranked as the most in-demand individual tech feature in current buyer surveys.
Integrated Security System
Smart locks, sensors, and app-controlled monitoring are expected to be pre-installed rather than buyer-sourced.
Dreamscape includes the Qolsys wireless system as standard.
Wired Connectivity
Buyers expect a structured wiring backbone: ethernet cabling throughout and in-wall USB and USB-C charging outlets.
The Views includes ethernet cabling and USB-C outlets as standard.
Top 5 Upgrades & Incentives
Full Appliance Package
Buyers expect to move in with all appliances included; the package is the most common point of comparison at any new home site.
Homeward Hills, Winchester, and Dreamscape offer 5–6 appliances in their current programs.
Design Centre Credit
Decor allowances now commonly run into the tens of thousands in the new construction market; buyers expect personalization dollars at signing.
Minto HOH East offers up to $37K; The Views offers $10K–$20K depending on lot width.
Development Charge Cap
Buyers want certainty on closing costs; DC caps have become a standard ask at new home sites in this price range.
Winchester caps at $7,500; Minto is currently offering $0 DCs.
EV Charger Rough-In
Buyers want infrastructure ready even without a current EV; increasingly described as a baseline expectation at this price point.
Winchester and Dreamscape include conduit to garage as standard.
Free Assignment
Provides flexibility for life changes and is a key consideration for investor purchasers; increasingly expected in new construction.
Winchester and Dreamscape both offer assignment with admin and legal fees.
Top 5 Standard Features
Outdoor Living Space
Ontario buyers consistently rank a covered deck or porch as a top priority; outdoor areas are viewed as an extension of the home itself.
Homeward Hills, Heartwood, and Minto HOH East include a pressure-treated deck where grade permits.
9' Ceilings Throughout
"No one is requesting shorter ceilings" is how one 2026 home design report put it. Nine-foot second-floor ceilings are now a buyer expectation.
Four of six competitors in this comp set offer 9' on the second floor.
Finished Basement Option
Ontario builders cite this as a consistently top-requested feature; a finished basement adds usable space for families without a post-close renovation.
Minto, Dreamscape, and Homeward Hills offer finished basement as an incentive or upgrade option.
Stone or Quartz Throughout
Buyers expect consistent finish quality across the home; quartz in both kitchen and bathrooms is the standard expectation at this price range.
Several competitors include stone countertops in the primary ensuite as standard.
Energy Efficiency Certification
Ontario buyers look for homes built to recognized standards; ENERGY STAR certification is cited as a key buyer confidence signal in 2026.
Homeward Hills is ENERGY STAR certified; Dreamscape includes HRV and dehumidifier as standard.

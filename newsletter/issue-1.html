What Sold in Your Community

What is True DOM?

The Listed DOM on MLS counts only the days on the most recent listing. When a seller terminates a listing and re-lists later, the clock resets to zero. True DOM adds up every active listing period for the property, giving you the full picture of how long a home has actually been trying to sell.

What Did Not Sell

The Listing That Expired

One home in the community has come off the market unsold this year. This is worth understanding, because it tells you as much about pricing strategy as the sold data does.

873 Rexton Dr

Listed at $1,028,000 on a 36 x 98 foot lot (4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms). The listing expired after 166 days on market without a sale. That is nearly five and a half months. In the same period, Tanglewood's other 36' homes that did sell closed at $1,050,000 and $1,100,000 — but both of those sellers went through significant price adjustments to get there. The lesson: the market is transacting, but the gap between where sellers want to be and where buyers are willing to go is real, and it costs time.

A straight take on where things stand

I will be honest about what the data is showing. The three homes that sold in Tanglewood this year all sold below their original list prices. Two of them sold at meaningful discounts. The one that sold with the least drama — 2143 Hallandale at -4.5% off its final list — was the one that came back to market at a price that reflected where buyers were actually prepared to transact.

This is not unique to Tanglewood. It reflects a broader reset across the GTA new-home market from the peak of 2022. What it means for you depends entirely on your situation: your timeline, your mortgage, your goals, and whether you are looking at your home as a place to live, a property to hold, or an asset to eventually leverage into something else.

I am not here to tell you what to do. I am here to make sure you have the right information to make that call yourself. If you want to talk through what your home is worth in this market, what it would realistically sell for versus what you paid, or whether holding and renting makes more sense than selling — that is a conversation I am happy to have.

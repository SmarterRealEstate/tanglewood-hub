What is True DOM?

Listed DOM is what you see on the MLS listing. True DOM is what actually happened. When a home is terminated and re-listed, the clock resets on paper but not in reality. True DOM adds up every listing period so you get the honest picture of how long a property was actually on the market.

New Home Market Report
North Oshawa New PreCon Home Market: Current Pricing

Builder list pricing, before HST. As of August 2026.

Whether you are keeping an eye on the market, thinking about a move, or know someone looking for a new home in the area, here is where North Oshawa new construction sits right now across the main home categories.

Executive Townhomes and Terrace Homes

A growing number of master-planned communities in North Oshawa offer townhome and terrace-style homes as an entry point into the area. Pricing in this category currently ranges from the high $600s to the low $800s, depending on the community, square footage, and level of finish. These typically run 1,400 to 1,900 sq ft above grade across 2 to 3 storeys.

30' Single-Garage Detached

The most common entry point into detached home ownership in Oshawa right now. Active communities including Dreamscape by Fieldgate, Winchester Estates by Menkes, Heartwood Village by Laurelcrest, and Heights of Harmony by Minto are pricing 30' lot homes from the mid-$700s to just under $960,000 before HST, depending on the model and square footage. Most homes in this range are 1,600 to 2,400 sq ft above grade.

36' Double-Garage Detached

The most sought-after category in the North Oshawa market right now. Communities currently selling on 36' lots include Heartwood Village by Laurelcrest, Heights of Harmony by Minto, The Views by Jeffery Homes, and Homeward Hills by Sorbara. Pricing runs from the low $960s to $1,275,000 before HST, with most well-positioned communities sitting between $960K and $1,170K. Square footage typically ranges from 1,900 to 3,000 sq ft above grade.

40'+ Double-Garage Detached

The premium tier in the area. Communities actively selling on 40' lots or larger include Heartwood Village by Laurelcrest, Homeward Hills by Sorbara, Dreamscape by Fieldgate (on 42' lots), and The Views by Jeffery Homes. Pricing currently ranges from $1,080,000 to just under $1,400,000 before HST, with most homes in the 2,500 to 3,150 sq ft above grade range.

Community Watch

What is Happening in the Area

Conlin Road Widening

Conlin Road East is being widened and reconstructed from east of Harmony Road North to Kurelo Drive. The two-year project started August 2025 and is set to complete December 2026. A new roundabout at Grandview Street North is completing this summer. New multi-use pathways and sidewalks are part of the scope.

New School in Kedron

A new public elementary school serving the north Oshawa area opened in September 2026, backed by a $30.8 million provincial investment with 680 student spaces. The Tanglewood site plan also shows a public elementary school reserved within the community itself for future phases.

Still No Traffic Light at Harmony and Britannia

I know it is a pain getting onto Harmony Road. I am keeping an eye out for any news on a signal at that intersection, and I am sure most of you are too. If you want to push the process along, give Durham Region a call at 311. That is the number to log concerns about roads and traffic signals, and a few more calls never hurts.

Jeff's Corner

What My Clients Are Asking Right Now

The question I am getting most often this summer is whether now is a good time to sell. Honest answer: it depends entirely on your number and your timeline.

The market has stabilized. Rates are at a three-year low. Buyers are active but selective. If you price correctly and the home shows well, you will find a buyer. If you are anchored to 2022 numbers, you will sit.

There is also an angle worth thinking about if you are selling to move up. The same market conditions that keep your sale price below 2022 apply equally to what you are buying next. What you give up on the sell side, you may recover on the purchase. That math is worth running before you decide.

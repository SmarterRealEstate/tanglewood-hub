True Days on Market

Every time a listing expires and relists, the MLS clock resets to zero. A home that has been sitting unsold for 14 months can show up in your search looking like it listed yesterday.

True DOM adds every listing period together. It shows you how long a home has actually been trying to sell, not just how long the current listing has been running.

Behind every long DOM number is the same short story. The price was wrong, the marketing missed, or both. Get those two things right from day one and the clock barely moves.

What This Trade War May Mean for Our Real Estate Market

The headlines have been loud. What matters is what is actually happening on the ground in North Oshawa, and what it means for the decision you may be thinking about.

Market Perspective

On the new construction side: Tariffs on lumber, steel, and building materials are pushing input costs higher for builders across the country. Medallion and other local builders are watching their cost structures closely. Some developers are moving cautiously on new phases, which tends to constrain supply over time. Less new supply in a market that is already under-built is generally supportive of existing home values. The homes that have already been built (like yours) benefit from this dynamic.

On the resale side: Buyer hesitation is real. Uncertainty creates pause. But pause is not the same as retreat. In North Oshawa, 30 homes sold in the first eight months of the year at 99% of asking on average. Well-priced homes are moving. The ones sitting at 50, 100, or 150+ days are almost uniformly the ones that started too high and chased the market down instead of getting ahead of it.

For sellers: This market rewards preparation, not optimism. The sellers getting deals done are pricing with discipline, presenting their homes well, and reaching the right buyer, not just any buyer. A few years ago, listing on MLS and waiting for the weekend worked. It does not work now. The data from your own neighbourhood tells that story clearly.

For buyers: Motivated sellers are in this market. The ones who have cut their prices and are still sitting are often open to conversations that would not have been possible in a stronger market. If you have been watching and waiting, this may be the right window.

The HST rebate window is closing. New homes purchased under an Agreement of Purchase and Sale signed between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027 qualify for the full federal HST rebate of up to $130,000 back on eligible purchases. This is one of the most meaningful buyer incentives in years, and it carries a hard deadline. If Phase 2 is on your radar, the timing matters.

Real estate has been Canada's most reliable long-term wealth builder across every economic cycle of the past century. That does not change because of tariff headlines. What changes is the tactics: buyers need more information to move with confidence, and sellers need better preparation to stand out. That is true in good markets and in uncertain ones.

New Home Market

North Oshawa new construction: current pricing

Builder list pricing, before HST, as of September 2026. Since Issue No. 1, Dreamscape by Fieldgate has moved its pricing. Most other communities are holding. Always confirm directly before acting on any number here.

Executive Townhomes and Terrace Homes: Communities across North Oshawa are pricing townhome and terrace-style product from the high $600s to the low $800s, depending on the community, square footage, and level of finish. Most run 1,400 to 1,900 sqft above grade across two to three storeys.

30' Single-Garage Detached: Active communities including Dreamscape by Fieldgate, Winchester Estates by Menkes, Heartwood Village by Laurelcrest, and Heights of Harmony by Minto are pricing 30-foot lot homes from the mid-$700s to just under $960,000 before HST. Most homes in this range are 1,600 to 2,400 sqft above grade.

36' Double-Garage Detached: The most sought-after category in the area. Communities selling on 36-foot lots include Heartwood Village, Heights of Harmony, The Views by Jeffery Homes, and Homeward Hills by Sorbara. Pricing runs from the low $960s to $1,275,000 before HST, with most well-positioned communities between $960K and $1,170K. Square footage typically runs 1,900 to 3,000 sqft above grade.

40'+ Double-Garage Detached: The premium tier. Communities actively selling on 40-foot lots or larger include Heartwood Village, Homeward Hills, Dreamscape (on 42-foot lots), and The Views. Pricing currently ranges from $1,080,000 to just under $1,400,000 before HST, with most homes in the 2,500 to 3,150 sqft above grade range.

Community Watch

What is happening in and around the neighbourhood

Conlin Road East Phase 2 Reconstruction

A $40 million infrastructure investment is underway on Conlin Road East, covering storm sewer upgrades, catch basin improvements, and drainage from Ritson Road North to Harmony Road North. This is a multi-year project that supports the Kedron growth plan and signals long-term investment in the area your home sits in. Project updates at oshawa.ca.

New Elementary School in Kedron

A new public elementary school backed by a $30.8 million provincial investment opened in September 2026 with 680 student spaces. Kedron is planned to absorb approximately 22,000 new residents over the coming years, with schools and commercial services built into the planning area.

Still no traffic light at Harmony and Britannia. If you want to push the process along, 311 is the number to log traffic signal concerns with Durham Region.

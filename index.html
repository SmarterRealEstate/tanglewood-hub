About the Community

Built for the Way Families Actually Live

Tanglewood is a master-planned community by Medallion Developments, located in North Oshawa near Highway 401 and 407, with quick access to GO Transit and VIA Rail. Phase 1 is fully sold out and occupied. Families have moved in, neighbours have met each other, and the community is exactly what was promised.

Phase 2 is coming. It picks up where Phase 1 left off: thoughtfully designed streetscapes, flexible home layouts, and a genuine neighbourhood feel that is rare in new developments. Details are still being finalized, but the foundation is solid and the demand is there.

This page is your permanent Tanglewood resource hub. As Phase 2 details become available, this is where you will find them first.

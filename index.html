The Neighbourhood

Everything You Need.
Closer Than You Think.

Tanglewood sits in one of Oshawa's most connected and convenient pockets — with highway access, transit, waterfront, and amenities all within easy reach.

Transit & Connectivity

  • Hwy 401 & Hwy 407 — minutes away
  • Oshawa GO Station (Lakeshore East Line)
  • Direct GO train to Union Station — approx. 60–75 min
  • VIA Rail Oshawa Station
  • Durham Region Transit throughout
  • Easy access to all major Durham arterials

Amenities & Lifestyle

  • Lake Ontario waterfront & Lakeshore parks
  • Oshawa Centre — major retail destination
  • Ontario Tech University & Durham College
  • Costco, big-box retail, and restaurant corridors
  • Proximity to Whitby and Ajax for additional options
  • Schools, parks, and recreation within the community

The Oshawa Growth Story

Oshawa is one of the fastest-growing cities in the GTA — and one of the last places where a family can still buy a detached home at a reasonable price. Infrastructure investment, a growing post-secondary population (Ontario Tech and Durham College together enroll tens of thousands of students), and a downtown core undergoing real revitalization all point in the same direction.

For buyers who got into Phase 1 of Tanglewood, the story has already played out well. For Phase 2, the fundamentals are the same — but the window is shorter. Oshawa's affordability advantage relative to Toronto, Mississauga, and even Whitby is narrowing. Tanglewood Phase 2 is an opportunity to get in before the gap closes.
